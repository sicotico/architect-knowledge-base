### 🧩 **1.2. Gramática Relevante / Grammar Review**

---

#### 📌 *1.2.1. Present Simple* – **Presente Simple**

| Forma         | Ejemplo                                         |
|---------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Afirmativa    | *She works at a tech company.*                   |
| Negativa      | *He doesn’t like programming.*                   |
| Interrogativa | *Do you study Computer Engineering?*             |

📚 *Usos comunes:* Rutinas, verdades generales, opiniones, hechos científicos.

---

#### ⏳ *1.2.2. Present Continuous* – **Presente Continuo**

| Forma         | Ejemplo                                              |
|---------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Afirmativa    | *I am studying for the exam.*                        |
| Negativa      | *She isn’t working today.*                           |
| Interrogativa | *Are you writing code now?*                          |

📚 *Usos comunes:* Acciones en curso, planes futuros ya programados.

---

#### ⚙️ *1.2.3. Modal Verbs* – **Verbos Modales (can, could, should)**

| Modal     | Uso                         | Ejemplo                                             |
|-----------|------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| **Can**   | Habilidad / Permiso / Posibilidad | *I can solve this problem.* / *Can I go now?*      |
| **Could** | Habilidad pasada / Más cortés | *Could you help me with this file?*                |
| **Should**| Consejo / Recomendación      | *You should update your software.*                 |
