The correct answer is:  

**Second conditional.**  

### Explanation:  
The **Second Conditional** is used to talk about **hypothetical or unlikely situations in the present or future**.  

**Structure:**  
✅ *If + past simple*, *would + base verb*  

**Example:**  
- *If I had money, I would go to Turkey.* (But I don’t have money, so I can’t go.)  

Other conditionals:  
- **Zero Conditional:** General truths (e.g., *If you heat water, it boils.*)  
- **First Conditional:** Real future possibilities (e.g., *If I have time, I will call you.*)  
- **Third Conditional:** Hypothetical past situations (e.g., *If I had studied, I would have passed the exam.*)
